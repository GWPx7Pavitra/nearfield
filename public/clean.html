The Science

According to landmark research by Berkeley Earth (Dr. Richard Muller & Elizabeth Muller), breathing air with an average PM₂.₅ of 22 µg/m³ for 24 hours deposits the same particulate mass deep into lung alveoli as smoking one cigarette.

Understanding Your Water & Air Values

BIS IS 10500:2012 Water Quality

Total Hardness & Minerals

Total hardness reflects calcium and magnesium ions. While hard water poses no direct cardiovascular hazard, excessive hardness encrusts pipes and degrades heating elements.

Acceptable: < 200 mg/L Permissible: 600 mg/L
BIS IS 10500:2012 Chemical Safety

Fluoride, Arsenic & Nitrates

Chemical contaminants with acute biological significance. Fluoride above 1.5 mg/L causes crippling skeletal fluorosis. Arsenic above 0.01 mg/L is a confirmed carcinogen.

Fluoride: 1.0 — 1.5 mg/L Arsenic: 0.01 mg/L max
CPCB NAQI Air Quality

National Air Quality Index

Calculated using 24-hour concentration sub-indices for PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, and SO₂ based on official CPCB health break-point matrices. 0–50 is Good; > 200 is Poor or hazardous.

Good: 0 — 50 Severe: > 400
