The Science Behind This Metric

According to landmark epidemiological research by Berkeley Earth (Dr. Richard Muller & Elizabeth Muller), breathing air with an average PM₂.₅ concentration of 22 µg/m³ for 24 hours deposits roughly the same microscopic particulate mass deep into lung alveoli as smoking one cigarette.

Equivalent Cigarettes = Daily PM₂.₅ (µg/m³) ÷ 22
Actionable Health Measures:

  • Wear a certified N95 or FFP2 respirator for outdoor commutes.
  • Avoid outdoor cardio exercise during morning traffic peaks and temperature inversions.

Understanding Your Water & Air Values

BIS IS 10500:2012 Water Quality

Total Hardness & Minerals

Total hardness reflects calcium and magnesium ions. While hard water poses no direct cardiovascular hazard, excessive hardness encrusts pipes, degrades heating elements, and reduces soap lather.

Acceptable Limit: < 200 mg/L Permissible: 600 mg/L
BIS IS 10500:2012 Chemical Safety

Fluoride, Arsenic & Nitrates

Chemical contaminants with acute biological significance. Fluoride above 1.5 mg/L causes crippling dental or skeletal fluorosis. Arsenic above 0.01 mg/L is a confirmed carcinogen.

Fluoride: 1.0 — 1.5 mg/L Arsenic: 0.01 mg/L max
CPCB NAQI Air Quality

National Air Quality Index

Calculated using 24-hour concentration sub-indices for PM₂.₅, PM₁₀, NO₂, and SO₂ based on official CPCB health break-point matrices. 0-50 represents Good air; > 200 indicates Poor or hazardous air.

Good: 0 — 50 Severe: > 400
